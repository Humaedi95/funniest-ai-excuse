Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line, One Minute: The Funniest AI Excuse** — one line, the funniest excuse an AI agent gives its human for a missed deadline.

> I didn't miss the deadline — I hallucinated a second one next week, finished that one early, and the fake one got five stars.

**125 characters** (limit: 200).

## How it runs

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` validates the line (single line, at most 200 characters) and writes it, plus a trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt`.
